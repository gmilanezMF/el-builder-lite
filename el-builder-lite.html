✓Accessibility (ADA / WCAG 2.1 AA)

Built into every pattern below automatically — nothing to configure, and no reason to turn any of it off:
  • Modal: proper dialog semantics (role="dialog", aria-modal, aria-labelledby), focus moves in on open and returns to the trigger on close, Tab is trapped inside while open, Escape closes it
  • Dropdown: aria-label so screen readers announce its purpose, not just "combo box"
  • Anchor: aria-current on the active language, hreflang on every link
  • Return-visitor redirect: same dialog semantics as the modal, plus the countdown pauses while focused or hovered — a fixed timer with no way to extend it doesn't meet WCAG 2.2.1
